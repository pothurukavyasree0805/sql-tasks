2. Logical Operators
Task: Given an employees table with columns name, salary, department, and city:

+--------+----------+----------+------------+-----------+
| emp_id | emp_name | salary   | department | city      |
+--------+----------+----------+------------+-----------+
|    101 | John     | 40000.00 | HR         | Hyderabad |
|    102 | Alex     | 42000.00 | Sales      | Pune      |
|    103 | James    | 62000.00 | Sales      | Hyderabad |
|    104 | James    | 56000.00 | Finance    | Hyderabad |
+--------+----------+----------+------------+-----------+
Task

Find employees in the 'Sales' department whose salary is greater than 40000

output:

 MySQL  localhost:33060+ ssl  d13  SQL > select emp_id, emp_name from employees
                                      ->  where department="sales" and salary>40000;
+--------+----------+
| emp_id | emp_name |
+--------+----------+
|    102 | Alex     |
|    103 | James    |
+--------+----------+


Find employees who are either from 'Hyderabad' or have a salary greater than 60000

output:

 MySQL  localhost:33060+ ssl  d13  SQL > select emp_id, emp_name from employees
                                      -> where city="Hyderabad" or salary>60000;
+--------+----------+
| emp_id | emp_name |
+--------+----------+
|    101 | John     |
|    103 | James    |
|    104 | James    |
+--------+----------+

Find all employees who do not belong to the 'Finance' department

output:

MySQL  localhost:33060+ ssl  d13  SQL > select emp_id, emp_name from employees
                                      -> where department!="Finance";
+--------+----------+
| emp_id | emp_name |
+--------+----------+
|    101 | John     |
|    102 | Alex     |
|    103 | James    |
+--------+----------+
